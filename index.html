About the Artist

Photographer

Drift Bottle Studio is led by an independent photographer focused on luxury portraits, wedding documentary work, and commercial imagery. The style emphasizes clean color, steady emotion, and clear visual storytelling, allowing people and scenes to become the natural center of the frame.

Services are primarily based in Florida, covering Miami, Orlando, Tampa, and nearby cities, with destination shoots available across the United States by project. Every project includes style planning, location guidance, and delivery expectations before the shoot.

Client Q&A

Client FAQ

When will the images be delivered?

Selected previews are usually delivered within 3-5 business days. Final retouched galleries are typically delivered in 2-4 weeks, depending on project scope.

Do you travel for shoots?

Yes. Shoots outside Florida are quoted separately based on travel, lodging, and schedule requirements.

How do I reserve a date?

After you submit the inquiry form, the photographer will reply by email with shoot recommendations, pricing, and available dates. A deposit confirms the booking.

Do you provide styling or location guidance?

Portrait and commercial projects can include wardrobe, hair and makeup, location, and reference-image guidance, with outside styling teams available as needed.
