# Drift Bottle Studio SEO Strategy

## Summary

Drift Bottle Studio is a local/service-area photography brand. The first SEO goal is to rank and convert for high-intent service searches such as high-end portrait photographer, wedding photographer, commercial photographer, and event photographer in the served cities.

## Core Pages

- `/` as the primary portfolio and conversion page.
- Future service pages: `/services/portrait-photography`, `/services/wedding-photography`, `/services/commercial-photography`, `/services/event-photography`.
- Future local pages only for real service areas with unique content, such as New York and New Jersey.

## On-Page SEO

- Lead with original portfolio images, service categories, pricing ranges, photographer bio, FAQ, and inquiry form.
- Use `ProfessionalService` schema now; expand to `Service`, `FAQPage`, and `ImageObject` schema when real image metadata is available.
- Keep NAP consistent once phone/address/service-area details are finalized.

## Content Roadmap

- Month 1: publish complete core services and replace placeholder visuals with original work.
- Month 2: add case-study style galleries for portrait, wedding, and commercial projects.
- Month 3: add location-specific pages only for cities actually served.

## KPIs

- Inquiry form submissions.
- Organic impressions and clicks for photography service keywords.
- Portfolio engagement and gallery clicks.
- Core Web Vitals pass rate on mobile.
